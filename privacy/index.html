Privacy Policy

Effective 31 August 2026

The short version. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers. Our Pinterest application connects only to our own Pinterest account — it never reads any other person’s Pins, boards or profile.

Who we are

PIXLFORGE Creative Studio is an independent digital products studio. We design and sell downloadable digital products — printable planners, wall art, templates, prompt packs and eBooks — through our Etsy shop, and we promote them on Pinterest.

1. This website

These are static informational pages. We set no cookies, run no analytics, embed no third-party trackers, and there are no accounts to create. We collect no personal information from visitors here.

This site is hosted on Google Sites. As part of serving any website, our hosting provider may log standard technical request data such as IP address and browser type. That processing is governed by the provider’s own privacy statement, not by us.

2. Our Pinterest application

We operate a private application that connects to our own Pinterest business account for a single purpose: publishing and organising our own marketing content. It is used by PIXLFORGE Creative Studio alone and is not offered to, or usable by, anyone else.

What it does

  • Creates and schedules Pins on boards we own
  • Creates and organises those boards
  • Reads performance data (saves, clicks, impressions) for our own Pins and boards

What it does not do

  • Access, read or store any other Pinterest user’s Pins, boards, profile or personal data
  • Serve any user other than PIXLFORGE Creative Studio itself
  • Run advertising campaigns
  • Share Pinterest data with any third party

Access tokens for our own account are stored locally on our own hardware and are not transmitted to anyone.

3. If you buy from our Etsy shop

Purchases are made and paid for on Etsy. Etsy, not us, processes your payment and holds your payment details — we never see your card information.

Etsy provides us with the limited order information we need to fulfil and support your purchase, which may include your name, your billing or delivery country, and your Etsy username or contact email.

We use that information only to deliver your download, answer your questions and handle refunds or disputes. We keep it only as long as we need it for customer support, business records and tax obligations. We do not add you to marketing lists, and we do not sell or share your information.

Etsy’s own handling of your data is governed by Etsy’s privacy policy.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact us and we will respond. For information Etsy holds about your purchase, you can also make requests directly to Etsy.

5. Children

Our products and shop are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about information we hold, can be sent to pixlforgellc@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date above.
